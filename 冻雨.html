About This Video

Freezing rain is a common hazardous weather phenomenon in spring and winter in Guizhou, China. When freezing rain falls on objects below 0 °C, it changes from water to ice. This video explains an important reason why freezing rain often occurs in Guizhou and introduces what a quasi-stationary front is.
