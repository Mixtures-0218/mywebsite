A LITTLE ABOUT ME

I am a University of Washington, Seattle, Class of 2027 undergraduate student pursuing double majors in Atmospheric & Climate Science: Meteorology and Atmospheric & Climate Science: Data Science, with a minor in Applied Mathematics (current GPA 3.81/4.0). My research interests focus on high-impact weather, machine learning, and mesoscale meteorology.

Currently, I am working as the lead undergraduate researcher with Professor Alexandra Anderson-Frey on evaluating Transformer-based machine learning weather models (Pangu-Weather, FengWu, FuXi) in reproducing mesoscale tornado-favorable convective environments. A first-author manuscript is in preparation, with submission targeted for October 2026.

In summer 2025, I interned as a software developer at the Sichuan Meteorological Bureau, automating the data-reporting pipeline from the provincial meteorological bureau to the provincial energy bureau, reducing a single task's processing time from 40 minutes to 15 seconds. I am also honored to receive the Sally Schoenberg and Randy Poteet Scholarship (2025-2026 & 2026-2027) and the Mindlin Scholarship (2026-2027).

Outside the classroom, I actively engage in meteorological science communication, running an educational channel on Bilibili with over 2,600 followers and more than 300,000 views. I also joined the school's DawgCast weather forecasting club, practicing forecasting skills with other meteorology enthusiasts. If you are interested in my research or science communication work, please feel free to contact me via leoluo@uw.edu.
