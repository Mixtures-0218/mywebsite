About This Video

Graupel, also known as snow pellets or soft hail, is a common form of precipitation in southern China in autumn and winter; it is not snow. Hail, on the other hand, is a common natural hazard in summer. The two look very similar and are easily confused. This video introduces graupel and hail and lists their differences for easy identification.
